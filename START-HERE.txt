LANGUINI v0.38.0 · by Mr. Saymaz

Extract the entire ZIP into one folder. Open the root index.html with its adjacent files and folders intact. Do not open source/public/index.html or play inside a ZIP preview.

For mobile play, upload the ready root files to Netlify or another static host, then open the hosted address in Safari or Chrome. Keep a stable address for saved progress.

WHAT CHANGED
Sentence blocks now follow grammatical roles rather than an exact three-piece limit. Complete subjects, verb groups, objects and place/time phrases stay intact. Preparation and washing use the actual count; see SENTENCE-BLOCKS.md.

Four additional grade banks add 1,772 activities and 44 sorting packs. Duplicates reuse existing practice; new subtopics stay under their appropriate parent units. See LANGUAGE-EXPANSION.md.

Preparation now reveals Turkish only after a correct English sentence, for two protected seconds. Washing uses 3/6/9 sentence plates in Cafe/Diner/Bistro. Later restaurants add fuller service routes, more menu/check activities and larger delivery routines.

Automatic tap-to-send tickets, the 3D review card, shorter receipt reading, revised delivery proportions, three preparation phases, tab recovery and sequential venue unlocks are retained.

phone-preview.html runs station demonstrations with separate temporary progress. The full game uses the browser's saved player progress.
