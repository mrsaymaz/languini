# Languini v0.34.0 · Learning progression

Built directly from the verified v0.33.0 release. Preserves the requested v0.26.0 rollback lineage, existing curriculum, saved progress, three-phase food construction and recent presentation upgrades.

## Preparation: meaning after success

Before a preparation sentence is correctly ordered, its Turkish meaning is absent from the visible/accessibility text. Hints still guide the next English block. Wrong attempts and partial answers do not expose Turkish.

A correct full sentence reveals its reviewed Turkish meaning on the recipe note for 2,000 ms. The next ingredients arrive afterward. This applies to Build, Layer and Finish. Completed food layers remain on the same dish.

The reveal timer counts only while the correct meaning is available to read. It freezes for pause/tab changes, an unready camera and requested audio/replay. Rotation preserves the remaining reveal time. No extra student action is required.

Sorting deliveries add one card per heading in Diner and two in Bistro, capped by the selected unit's reviewed category bank. They stay one sorting batch rather than repeat the entire batch. Normal labels use separate parcels with their own correct choices.

Every extra assessment remains in the chosen grade/unit. No new themes, unreviewed sentence variants, distractor sentence blocks or unrelated vocabulary were introduced. A few units have six distinct sentence answers; a nine-plate routine uses all available answers before returning to the least recently used ones. Consecutive plate sentences do not repeat.

## Washing and completion

Students see one dirty white serving plate at a time. Tap/swipe the three blocks in order, clean the plate, then continue to a fresh dirty plate with a new sentence. Plate number and total are visible throughout. Pause or rotation preserves the current plate and accepted blocks.

The physical dish inventory is conserved: the captured dirty rack is returned once after the entire routine, using the existing rinse. Completing an early sentence never credits the whole batch. Wash quality averages all plate assessments for the restaurant's Service review. Washing has no direct currency reward.

Menu/ticket/checkout rounds advance their order route once at the end. Nonfinal rounds do not pay another station reward, reserve stock again, start cooking or allow premature serving. Delivery restocks and pays once after the final parcel. Every round has its own protected freshness clock, and all order assessments contribute to customer review quality/speed.

Tap-to-fill/automatic ticket sending, the 3D review revolution, shorter printed-check reading, compact delivery paper and sequential Cafe → Diner → Bistro unlocks remain.
