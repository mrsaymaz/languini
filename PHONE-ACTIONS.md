# Clear phone actions · v0.52.0

Phone-width screens (under 760 CSS px) and coarse-pointer devices use one contextual main button: Cook for a prepared tray, Serve T1 for a collected meal, Restock for a labeled crate, or the selected task's next action. Hold and Return are absent from this phone dock and from nearby phone badges. Other available jobs remain selectable through their location badges and tickets. The existing floor ring and route show the current destination; its nearby badge has a quiet outline. Desktop mouse layouts retain the explicit station dock. The existing Tea control remains accessible in the service UI.

Choosing another job while carrying something walks the chef to storage first. A prepared tray or supply crate goes on the holding counter. A finished meal goes back to the serving pass, retaining its serving dish and order identity. Only after arrival does the ordinary command begin the chosen job. No English answer, cooking, service reward or supply reward is completed by storage. A stored item has a Collect badge at its counter slot. Two-slot counters expose both items individually.

If the holding counter is full, selecting a stored tray swaps it with the carried tray at the counter. Other switches requiring free hands remain unavailable until space is freed; the carried tray's Cook or crate's Restock action remains available. Pausing freezes the route. Choosing another route cancels the abandoned continuation. Stale order identities cannot open replacement customers' tasks. Serving plate counts and the trolley's two-meal capacity remain intact.
