How to play

  1. Take an order

    Tap a guest or ticket.

    Complete the English, then carry the tray to Cook.

    Collect at Pass, then tap the matching ticket.

Service pauses for English. Hints are free.

Kitchen & controls

Supplies → Receiving → Pantry. Leave a load at Counter. Tap or swipe sentence blocks at Sink to wash dishes.

Tap the floor to walk. On a keyboard, use WASD or arrows; E follows the suggested action. Space works when no button is focused.
