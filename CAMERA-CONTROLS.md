# Restaurant camera · v0.57.0

Phones (portrait, narrower than 760px) start each live shift at 140% and the camera follows the chef, keeping them in the middle of the playable area. Pinching, dragging or the −/+ buttons hand the camera to the player and stop following; Reset returns to following. Tapping an activity mark shows its source and holds the view until the chef sets off somewhere new. Desktop keeps the whole-room view.

Desktop: use the mouse wheel over the restaurant or −/+. Phone: pinch the restaurant, or open the camera button for −/+. Zoom stays between 90% and 140%. At closer zoom, drag empty floor to pan within the restaurant; a short tap still walks normally. Reset restores the default view; phones show a quick-reset button after the player moves the camera themselves.

Plate: meal ready. Customer: order needs attention. Bag: takeaway needs attention. Crate: supplies needed. Arrows point towards their source; counts combine off-screen orders of the same kind. Urgent orders briefly pulse amber, with motion disabled under Reduced motion. Tapping a mark reveals its source without moving the chef or starting work. Where a clamped pan cannot reveal the source, the view widens to 90%.

Only unresolved activity outside the usable restaurant field receives a mark. The field excludes ticket, camera and lower HUD areas. Floating guidance yields to activity marks so the playfield stays clear. English tasks, pause, story and setup hide the controls and marks; language cameras and timing stay protected. A completed task restores the chosen overview; a new shift resets it. No camera preferences or gameplay events are sent to another service.
